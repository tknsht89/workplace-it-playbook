# First-Line Troubleshooting for Online Meeting Audio Dropouts

## Purpose

Provide a practical first-line process for identifying whether online meeting audio dropouts are more likely related to Wi-Fi, the endpoint device, the internet connection, or the meeting platform.

## Intended User

Office IT support staff, small-business operators, and workplace users who need to begin troubleshooting an audio issue during an online meeting.

## Problem

Audio may cut out, become distorted, or lag during an online meeting. The first goal is not to guess the cause, but to compare conditions and narrow down the likely source of the issue.

## First-Line Checks

1. Confirm whether the issue occurs on Wi-Fi only or also on wired Ethernet.
2. Test with another device if one is available.
3. Check CPU and memory usage while the meeting is running.
4. Confirm whether the same issue occurs on another meeting platform.
5. Check whether large uploads, backups, cloud synchronization, or software updates are running.
6. Confirm whether multiple users experience the issue at the same time.

## How to Interpret Results

- Wi-Fi only: Check signal quality, access point placement, radio congestion, interference, and roaming behavior.
- Wired and Wi-Fi: Check endpoint performance, shared internet connectivity, or the meeting platform.
- One device only: Check the device, audio driver, headset, browser, and background processes.
- Multiple devices at the same time: Check shared network conditions, WAN connectivity, or meeting platform status.
- One meeting only: Check meeting-specific settings, participants, and the platform service status.

## Next Action

Record the connection type, device type, symptom, test performed, and result. Use anonymized records only, then escalate with the relevant evidence if the issue continues.

## Safety and Privacy

Do not publish real company names, customer information, SSIDs, IP addresses, WAN details, internal network diagrams, meeting recordings, account information, or confidential troubleshooting logs.
